Cancer stem cells (CSCs) have the ability to dictate tumor initiation, recurrence, and metastasis. Here, we examined the expression of aα2δ1+ in laryngeal cancer tissues and further determined the effect of α2δ1 on the migratory ability and tumorigenicity of laryngeal cancer cells. Immunofluorescence staining revealed that α2δ1 was positive in 13 (13/16, 81.25%) cases in laryngeal squamous cell carcinoma (LSCC) tissues, 7 (7/16, 43.75%) cases in paracancerous tissues and only 2 (2/16, 12.5%) cases in normal tumor tissues. Our quantitative RT-PCR assays further showed that α2δ1+ LSCC cells expressed significantly higher levels of stem cell-associated genes and drug efflux and resistance genes versusα2δ1- cells. Sphere-forming assays demonstrated higher sphere-forming efficiency in the α2δ1+versusα2δ1- subpopulation. Our Matrigel assays showed that α2δ1+ cells exhibited significantly greater invasive and migratory ability than α2δ1- cells. Furthermore, the percentage of purified α2δ1+ in TU686 and TU212 cells treated cisplatin or paclitaxel was significantly higher than that of the control group. Tumor xenograft assays revealed that the tumorigenicity of α2δ1+ cells was much higher than α2δ1- cells. In conclusion, a α2δ1+ subpopulation with CSC-like property was present in laryngeal cancer and possessed high self-renewal activity and was sufficient for tumor growth, differentiation, migration, invasion, and chemotherapeutic resistance. They could represent a promising therapeutic target for LSCC.

Laryngeal cancer is the second most common head and neck malignancy. Globally, approximately 110,000 to 130,000 new cases are diagnosed each year [1]. In China, the crude incidence rate of laryngeal cancer was 1.86/100,000 between 2008 and 2012 and the crude mortality was 1.01/100,000 [2]. Mainstay treatments for laryngeal cancer include radiotherapy, chemotherapy and surgical resection; however, management of advanced laryngeal cancer is complex and a consensus therapeutic strategy has not yet emerged.
Cancer stem cells (CSCs) are implicated in tumor initiation and differentiation, and are of high self-renewal properties, and can drive the tumorigenic process. The ability of CSCs to drive cancer initiation and progression also make them prime targets for treatments [4]. Evidence suggests that a minor population of CSCs in laryngeal cancer is extremely tumorigenic and possesses the potential to differentiate into cells that are responsible for tumor propagation and relapse [4]. Laryngeal CSCs are characterized by the expression of aldehyde dehydrogenase isoform 1 (ALDH1) [5], CD133 [6], and CD44 [7, 8]. However, laryngeal CSCs appear to differ considerably and may contribute to the heterogeneity of laryngeal cancer [9]. The calcium channel α2δ1 subunit serves an important role in regulating calcium oscillation amplitude [10] and has been reported in hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) [11]. This subpopulation of HCC cells possesses stem cell-like properties. In comparison to the non-purified cells, the invasiveness ability, self-renewal ability and the expression level of genes related to stem cells were increased in α2δ1+ HCC cells [11, 12]. Knocking down the α2δ1 gene in combination with doxorubicin treatment compromises HCC oncogenesis [11]. Recently, Yu et al. have demonstrated that non-small cell lung cancer cells expressing α2δ1 demonstrated CSC-like properties, and may contribute to chemoresistance [13].
Currently, no study is available on the role of α2δ1 in laryngeal cancer. In the present study, we examined the expression of a α2δ1+ in laryngeal cancer tissues and further determined the effect of α2δ1 on the migratory ability and tumorigenicity of laryngeal cancer cells.
The current study included 3 major parts. In the first part that involved clinical samples, α2δ1 expression was compared between primary lesions versus non-cancerous adjacent tissues collected from 16 LSCC patients receiving larygectomy and no neoadjuvant therapy. The second part of the study was conducted using 2 representative laryngeal squamous cell carcinoma (LSCC) cell lines: TU212 (highly malignant) versus TU686 (less malignant). Briefly, α2δ1+ cells were purified using flow cytometry from cultured cells. The following features were compared between α2δ1+ and α2δ1- cells: 1) representative CSC markers (BMI1, SOX2, EPCAM, OCT4, CNTTB, KLF4, NANO, ABCG2 and MDR1); 2) sphere formation; 3) differentiation; 4) migration/invasion; 5) sensitivity to cisplatin/paclitaxel. These features were also examined after α2δ1+ knockdown using shRNAs in α2δ1+ cells. In the final step of xenograft experiments with NOD/SCID mice, the ability of α2δ1+ versus α2δ1- TU686 cells to form tumor and subsequent growth was compared using limited dilution.
Archived surgically resected laryngeal squamous cell carcinoma (LSCC) tissue specimens were obtained from 16 treatment-naive male patients and snap-frozen in liquid nitrogen. The study protocol was approved by the institute ethics committee of Beijing Friendship Hospital, Capital Medical University (no. 2017-P2-187-01) and written informed consent was obtained from all the study subjects.

The U6 promoter-driven shRNA expression cassettes were transferred to the lentiviral shuttle vector plenti6 (Invitrogen). Lentiviral packaging, infection, and selection of blasticidin-resistant cell pools were performed as previously described [24]. The sequences of human α2δ1-specific 29-mer shRNAs were as follows: shRNA53, 5’-ACTCAACTGGACAAGTGCCTTAGATGAAG-3’ and shRNA56, 5’-AGATGCAAGAAGACCTTGTCA CACTGGCA-3’. The culture medium was replenished with 200 μL supplementary media every other day. Spheroids (⩾ 100 μm) were counted using a microscope (Zeiss, Oberkochen, Germany) after 4 weeks. In addition, passage two α2δ1+ cells and α2δ1+ cells treated with shRNA53 and shRNA56 were used for sphere-formation assays where indicated.
α2δ1+ cells were seeded in a Petri dish containing 10 mL RPMI 1640 and 10% FBS and cultured at 37∘C in a humidified incubator with 5% CO2. After 1 week of culture, the percentage of α2δ1+ cells was calculated by flow cytometry.

Immunofluorescence staining revealed abundant α2δ1+ cells in LSCC tissues, but hardly any α2δ1+ cells in normal laryngeal tissues and occasional α2δ1+ cells in paracancerous tissues (Fig. 1A). Furthermore, α2δ1 was positive in 13 (13/16, 81.25%) cases in LSCC tissues, 7 (7/16, 43.75%) cases in paracancerous tissues and only 2 (2/16, 12.5%) cases in normal tumor tissues (Fig. 1B).
We isolated α2δ1+ and α2δ1- cells in TU686 cells via FACS. Our qRT-PCR assays showed that α2δ1+ cells expressed significantly higher levels of stem cell-associated genes versusα2δ1- cells: EpCAM (3.5 fold), OCT4 (2.3 fold), CNTTB1 (2.0 fold), KLF4 (1.7 fold), NANO (1.6 fold), and ABCG2 (1.5 fold) (all P< 0.05). In addition, the α2δ1+subpopulation expressed significantly higher levels of the drug efflux transporter gene BMI1 (4.5 fold) and the multidrug resistance gene SOX2 (3.8 fold) compared to α2δ1- cells (P< 0.05) (Fig. 2).
Sphere formation assays further showed that α2δ1+ cells formed a significantly higher number of spheres than α2δ1- cells (P< 0.05) (Fig. 3A–C). Furthermore, passage 2 α2δ1+ cells had significantly increased sphere-forming ability versus passage 1 α2δ1+ cells (Fig. 3D and E). Moreover, knocking down α2δ1 by α2δ1-specific shRNAs significantly inhibited the sphere forming ability of α2δ1+ cells (Fig. 3F).
After purified TU212 α2δ1+ cells were cultured for 1 week, the percentage of α2δ1+ cells decreased from 96.85% to 73.27%; the percentage of α2δ1+ TU686 cells decreased from 92.49% to 3.08% (Fig. 4), suggesting that α2δ1+ cells differentiated into α2δ1- cells.
Our Matrigel assays showed that α2δ1+ cells exhibited significantly greater invasive ability than α2δ1- cells (Fig. 5A–D). Furthermore, in comparison to α2δ1- cells, α2δ1+ cells showed significantly greater migratory ability (Fig. 5E).
We treated TU686 cells with cisplatin (6 μmol/L) or paclitaxel (0.2 μmol/L) for 72 hours. Our flow cytometric analysis showed the percentage of purified α2δ1+ cells in TU686 cells treated cisplatin or paclitaxel was significantly higher than that of the control group (cisplatin: 7.87% vs. control: 1.72%; paclitaxel:10.87% vs. control: 1.72%) (Fig. 6).
As few as 102-103 purified α2δ1+ cells from the TU212 and TU686 cell lines initiated tumor formation in almost all inoculated mice (Fig. 7A–E). By contrast, α2δ1- cells were completely non-tumorigenic or formed tiny nodules only occasionally (Fig. 7A–E). Passage 2 α2δ1+ and α2δ1- cells further confirmed that α2δ1+cells were consistently more tumorigenic than α2δ1- cells (Fig. 7F and G). As a matter of fact, mice inoculated with passage 2 α2δ1- cells showed no evident tumor formation at 12 weeks (Fig. 7F and G). Furthermore, shRNA53 and shRNA56 α2δ1+ knockdown significantly suppressed the of tumor xenografts from α2δ1+ TU686 cells (Fig. 7H–J).

α2δ1 is a voltage-gated calcium (Cav) channel subunit [25] that governs synaptic input integration, activation of calcium-dependent signaling cascades, neuronal migration, neurotransmitter, and hormone release [26]. Existing evidence shows that α2δ1 is expressed in HCC [10]. Knocking down α2δ1 induced apoptosis of tumor-initiating cells (TIC) and inhibited tumor formation and self-renewal capacities of HCC cells. In contrast, α2δ1 overexpression increased sphere formation. These results indicated that α2δ1+ HCC cells possess stem cell-like properties. In the current study, we found that α2δ1 was expressed in a subpopulation of laryngeal cancer TU212 and TU686 cells, and α2δ1+ LSCC cells possessed stem cell-like properties.
Immunofluorescence staining of 16 LSCC specimens showed that α2δ1+ was significantly more frequently expressed in laryngeal cancer tissue than that in paracancerous tissues and normal tissues. This result is consistent with previous findings in other malignancies [10, 13]. Next, we used qPCR to detect CSC-associated genes and drug resistance genes. The results showed high level of expression of stem cell-associated genes, including BMI-1 [4], SOX2 [27], EpCAM [28], OCT4 [29], CTNNB1 [30], and ABCG2 [31, 32, 33] in α2δ1+ TU686 cells. These data together showed that a CSC subpopulation in laryngeal cancer is marked by a surface phenotype α2δ1. Only α2δ1+ cells for this marker showed stem-cell like characteristics, including the ability to differentiate and for self-renewal, and the capability of resisting standard chemotherapy.
Next, we showed that TU212 and TU686 cells also contain a α2δ1+ subpopulation. Purified α2δ1+ cells had the capability to multiply as spheroids in growth factor-supplemented, serum-free medium under non-adherent conditions. After culturing for 4 weeks, cancer spheres of undifferentiated α2δ1+cells were obtained. In contrast, the sphere-forming ability of α2δ1- cells were much weaker than α2δ1+cells under the same conditions. Cell spheres deriving from single cells that were acquired from dissociated α2δ1+ cells showed nearly limitless growth capability and could be expanded in subsequent serial propagations. Therefore, we speculate that α2δ1 may play a crucial role in the sphere formation ability of TU212 and TU686 cells. This is consistent with a previous study [10], which found that α2δ1 Cav channel overexpression resulted in increased sphere formation in HCC Hep-11 and Hep-12 cells. Self-renewal ability is one of the characteristics of CSC; these results indicate that α2δ1+ cells in TU212 and TU686 cells may represent CSCs.
We next evaluated the differentiation and migration/invasion potential of the α2δ1+subpopulation in TU212 and TU686 cells. The results showed that α2δ1+ cells could differentiate into α2δ1- cells and α2δ1+ cells while α2δ1- cells did not have this property. α2δ1+ cells also had greater differentiation and migration/invasion potential than α2δ1-cells. The ability to differentiate and migrate/invade is also a feature of CSCs.
Consistent with a previous study on α2δ1+ cells in lung cancer [13], treatment with cisplatin and paclitaxel increased the percentage of α2δ1+cells in TU686 cells in our experiments, suggesting that laryngeal cancer cells expressing α2δ1 are resistant to chemotherapeutic agents. According to previous report [13], we believe that cisplatin/paclitaxel may cause α2δ1-cells to undergo apoptosis or necrosis, while α2δ1+ cells are insensitive to chemotherapeutic drugs and can survive the tumoricidal effect of cisplatin/paclitaxel. Therefore, the ratio of α2δ1+ cells to all viable cells increases, and the effect of enriching CSC is achieved. In future studies, we will verify the above speculation and grading the concentration of chemotherapeutic drugs to see if the change in the ratio of α2δ1+ cells is time- and dose-dependent in a range of drug concentrations. Available evidence indicates that α2δ1+ cells are resistant to cisplatin/paclitaxel. Resistance to radiotherapy and chemotherapy is one of the important biological characteristics of CSCs.
Previous studies have confirmed that α2δ1 plays a crucial role in regulating the amplitude of CSC calcium oscillations [10]. Usually, spontaneous calcium oscillations in cells are caused by excitable tissues such as muscles, nerve tissues, embryonic stem cells, and immature dendritic cells. In most cases, their regulatory mechanisms and biological functions have not been elucidated. Calcium oscillations, a major form of calcium signaling, can promote the expression of specific genes, which is related to the magnitude and duration of calcium transients. It is possible to bind to the enhancer site and initiate transcription by keeping the transcription factor in the nucleus at a sufficiently high level. We hypothesize that α2δ1 may be involved in the “amplitude-encoding” signal in LSCC, thereby maintaining the stem cell characteristics of LSCC.
Upon in vivo tumor xenograft assays, 100 purified α2δ1+ cells were sufficient for tumor formation. In contrast, α2δ1- cells either were completely non-tumorigenic or formed tiny nodules only occasionally. In vivo serial inoculation assays with re-sorted cells from tumors formed by implanted α2δ1+ cells confirmed the tumorigenesis potential of α2δ1+ but not α2δ1- cells. Knocking down α2δ1 in purified α2δ1+ cells using shRNA reduced sphere formation and decreased tumorigenic cells in TU686 cells. These data indicate that α2δ1 has an important influence on sphere formation or tumorigenicity of LSCC both in vivo and in vitro, and has the potential to become a LSCC CSC marker. Nevertheless, the role of α2δ1 in the onset, progression and metastasis of LSCC remains to be further studied.
In summary, the results from the current study indicated the presence of a α2δ1+ subpopulation with CSC-like property in laryngeal cancer. The specific mechanism of CSC-like effects exerted by α2δ1 in laryngeal cancer requires further study.
